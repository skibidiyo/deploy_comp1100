# Test Cards

## Hypothesis 1: University students struggle to make friends

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that making friends could be challenging for a lot of university students.

**TEST**  
To verify that we will interview university students to collect data and insights — identifying emotional barriers, current behaviours, preferences, and desired features in a platform.

**METRIC**  
And measure:
- The main reasons students avoid approaching others (e.g. fear of rejection, lack of conversation topics)  
- What has worked for them so far  
- Gaps in current solutions  
- Preferences for connecting with others  
- Trust factors (what helps them feel safe)  
- Challenges faced when trying to make friends  

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if:
- The majority of students face challenges in finding friends  
- There is dissatisfaction with current methods (clubs, events, apps)  
- There is a clear demand for low-pressure ways to make friends  
- Students consistently report difficulty forming connections at university
