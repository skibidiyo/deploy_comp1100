## Hypothesis 3: Group Interaction Preference Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that most university students prefer forming initial connections in group settings rather than strict one-on-one interactions.

**TEST**  
To verify this, we will analyze student responses regarding their preference between one-on-one and group connection scenarios.

**METRIC**  
And measure:
- Percentage of students preferring one-on-one vs. group settings
- Reasoning behind each preference
- Whether preference correlates with introversion/extroversion
- Whether group size matters    

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if:
- >50% of students express preference for group settings over strict one-on-one
- Students cite "less pressure," "less intimidating," or "others can help carry conversation" as reasons

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**  
1. Would you prefer connecting one-on-one or in groups? Why?  
2. What would make it easier for you to build friendships or find community at uni?
