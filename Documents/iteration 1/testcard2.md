## Hypothesis 2: Shared Interests Foster Friendships

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that university students are more likely to form friendships when they share common interests or activities.

**TEST**  
To verify this, we will analyze interview responses, specifically looking for how often shared interests are mentioned as a key factor in forming connections. We will focus on answers to questions about what helps build friendships and what information is important to know before connecting.

**METRIC**  
We will measure:
- The frequency of students mentioning shared interests, hobbies, or activities as important for friendship.
- How often students express a desire for features that connect them based on interests.

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if:
- A significant number of students state that common interests are a primary factor for making friends.
- Students repeatedly mention shared activities as a way they have successfully made friends.
- There is a demand for app features that help find others with similar interests.

We are wrong if:
- Most students do not mention shared interests as an important factor in forming friendships.
