## Customer Segments

General
- University students 

Specifically
- First-year students (high priority)
- Introverted or socially anxious students
- Students looking for interest-based friendships
- University clubs/societies (secondary)

## Value propositions
- Making it less challenging for introverted university students to find a community or make friends on campus.
- Provide both group-based and one-on-one connections
- It's better because we cater specifically to introverted university students compared to other applications which are more broad


## Channels
- Mobile app / website
- Advertisement
- Social Media

## Customer relationship
- Self-service: users create profiles, swipe, match, and chat by themselves
- Community-based: group events, forums, or shared-interest spaces help users feel connected
- Personalized engagement: notifications, friendship suggestions, and event recommendations keep users active

## Revenue streams
- University partnerships for events (i.e. O-week)
- Premium version for features (tinder style)
- Partnerships with University Clubs for advertising
