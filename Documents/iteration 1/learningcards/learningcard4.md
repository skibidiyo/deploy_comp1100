# Learning Card 4: Social anxiety/confidence barrier (Test Card 4 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
The majority of university students feel uncomfortable approaching new people due to social anxiety or confidence issues.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
Confirmed, but the core issue is context (cold unknown vs shared task). Confidence boosts when group structure exists and when they know where to start.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- Anxiety arises from potential rejection, awkwardness, or inappropriate timing (headphones, lectures)
- Avoiding strangers stems from not knowing what to talk about or fearing cultural miscues
- Structured activities (group projects, club events) allow calm approach, repeated contact, and reduces anxiety

## EVIDENCE FROM ALL STUDENTS BY SOURCE
- **Kai1**: “I’m introverted... I only approach at same interest class.”
- **Kai2**: “I don’t know the best way to approach someone.”
- **Anindiyo1**: “I worry about whether it will be awkward or if they’re not interested.”
- **Anindiyo2**: “They are already in group, they might not want me in; group dynamics.”
- **Serafina1**: “Headphones = don’t disturb; cultural differences make it stressful.”
- **Shenal #2 (Nadeesha)**: “In lectures everyone is in their space, awkward to randomly talk.”
- **Anya (Haruka)**: “I feel comfortable when there's a focused cohort; otherwise I hesitate.”
- **Anya (Lachlan)**: “I am shy and I prefer looking for interest flags before approaching.”
- **Anya (Sora)**: “Social anxiety is biggest if they seem busy or in a hurry.”
- **Anya (Thomas)**: “The large unfamiliar environment itself is intimidating; I rely on the 'third place' to reduce anxiety.”

## DECISION
**ACCEPTED (refined)**: Barrier confirmed. Mobilize design around anxiety-reducing contexts, not requiring students to become suddenly confident.

## NEXT STEP
- Provide contextual connectors (e.g., “hello, same assignment?” prompts)
- Add safety filters and introvert-friendly modes
- Offer confidence pathways: small steps, “warm onboarding peers”, response-reliability status
