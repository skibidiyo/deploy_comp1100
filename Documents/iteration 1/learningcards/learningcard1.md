# Learning Card 1: Students struggle making friends (Test Card 1 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
University students struggle to make friends due to social barriers, lack of context, and inconsistent connection paths.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
All transcripts in `Transcripts/` confirm this is true: students consistently report difficulty approaching strangers, fearing awkwardness, and needing structured, shared activities to start connecting.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- 100%: Approach anxiety or environment barrier (lecture halls, headphones, busy personal space)
- 90%: Prefer group settings or pre-defined tasks (assignments, club events, tutorials) as safe entry
- 80%: Desire ability to see shared interests or classes before reaching out
- 70%: Existing offerings (Discord/LINE/Tinder) are not optimized for finding new campus friends

## EVIDENCE FROM ALL STUDENTS BY SOURCE
- **Kai #1**: "I'm fairly introverted... if we’re in same class we have same interests." (needs shared context)
- **Kai #2**: "I pretty much just go to uni to get my classes done... don't know best way to approach someone." (needs a process)
- **Kai #3**: "I used to struggle to pull myself out there... joined running club and got better" (supports structured club path)
- **Anindiyo #1**: "I worry about awkwardness and whether they’re interested." (fear of rejection)
- **Anindiyo #2**: "If they’re already in a group, you feel they have friends; harder to approach." (group dynamics barrier)
- **Serafina #1**: "I avoid people with headphones; I don’t want to disrespect them." (context sensitivity)
- **Shenal #2 (Nadeesha)**: "Lectures are individual; everyone on phones; hard to talk." (environment not social)
- **Anya (Haruka)**: "If people are with their own groups or busy on phones, I hesitate to approach." (group/visibility barrier)
- **Anya (Lachlan)**: "I put headphones on and tunnel vision; I avoid talking before I see shared interest." (introvert focus)
- **Anya (Sora)**: "Social anxiety is biggest; people in groups or with phones are harder to start with." (anxiety + group blockage)
- **Anya (Thomas)**: "It's intimidating, big place, unfamiliar strangers; club spaces helped." (environment + need for third place)

## DECISION
**ACCEPTED** with refinement: The hypothesis is true, but struggle is heavily mitigated by context-based interventions (common tasks, events, small groups).
