Interview 4

[Recording][1]


Q: What usually stops you from approaching or talking to new people?
A: Usually, it’s the awkwardness. I’m scared that they might judge me or notice differences between us. I’m also quite shy and not used to talking to new people.

Q: What has helped you the most in building a network of friends so far?
A: Joining a club has helped me the most. Being around people with the same interests makes it easier to connect and get along while doing activities together.

Q: What makes it hard to approach other students?
A: I think it’s the fear of being judged. Since I don’t know them, I worry they might judge me based on my background or personality.

Q: What would make it easier for you to build friendships or find a community at uni?
A: Finding and joining communities, especially through the internet, helps. Being part of a community with shared interests makes it easier to connect with others.

Q: What is lacking from finding friends via clubs or university events?
A: Couldn't answer.

Q: Would you prefer connecting one-on-one or in groups?
A: I prefer one-on-one. In groups, it feels harder because there are many people, and I feel like everyone is looking at me.

Q: So are you an introvert or extrovert?
A: I would say I’m an introverted person, but I can be comfortable around people I already know.

Q: What kind of information would you want to know about someone before deciding to connect?
A: Whether they have the same interests as me, like similar hobbies or passions. That would make it easier to connect.

Q: Have you used any apps or group chats to connect with people?
A: Yes, mostly LINE and group chats within the same community.

Q: What did you dislike about that?
A: There is often a lack of information about people. For example, some don’t have profile pictures, so I don’t know what they look like or even basic details about them.

Q: If you could design an app to help you make friends, what features would you want and why?
A: I would focus on matching based on interests and hobbies. If people can connect through shared interests, it would make making friends much easier.

Q: What would make you feel comfortable reaching out to someone new?
A: Events would help, like regular (weekly or monthly) gatherings based on shared hobbies, so people can meet and interact more naturally.
