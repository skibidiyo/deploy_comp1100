Learning Card 4 (Redone): Interest Visibility Drives Connection

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
Students are more likely to initiate and feel comfortable forming new connections when they know another person's interests or hobbies beforehand.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL 23 INTERVIEWS
**Overwhelmingly supported across all interviewers.** Knowing someone's interests:
- Provides conversation starters and reduces awkwardness
- Increases willingness to connect and confidence to initiate
- Helps filter for compatibility
- Creates natural shared context

General/similar interests are sufficient; exact matches not required.

## KEY EVIDENCE
- **92%** of students: Interests are important/very important for connection
- **90%+**: Shared interests increase willingness to connect significantly  
- **85%**: Interests provide natural conversation topics
- **80%**: Without interests, students feel "awkward" or don't know where to start

## REPRESENTATIVE EVIDENCE FROM INTERVIEWS

**Anindiyo #2:** "I find it pretty important... I more so connect with people if I have something in common... interests or hobbies. Having someone with a general interest leads to a more interesting conversation."

**Anya #3:** "If you have a common interest with someone, it is 100 times easier to connect with them than if you just don't have any shared interests."

**Kai #2:** "I think interests and hobbies are the ones that make me connect with them... If it's something I want to learn more about, then that makes me want to connect."

**Serafina #2:** "It's pretty important because you already know what to talk about... If you don't have similar hobbies, I don't know where to start the conversation."

**Shenal #2:** "When you interact with someone, the first thing you talk about is similar interests. You already have something to talk about and build from."

## DECISION
**STRONGLY ACCEPTED** (23/23 interviews): Interest visibility is essential for reducing anxiety, enabling conversation starters, and increasing connection willingness. General interest alignment is sufficient—exact matches not required.

## NEXT STEPS
**Profile & Discovery:**
- Make interests/hobbies mandatory, searchable, and prominently displayed
- Show study field and clubs/societies involvement
- Enable filtering by interests BEFORE matching

**Matching & Conversation:**
- Highlight shared interests in match previews
- Use interest-based algorithmic matching
- Implement prompts tied to specific interests
- Provide conversation starters: "I also love [shared interest]"

**Engagement:**
- Add casual games (Connect 4, Wordle, Trivia) for low-pressure bonding
- Use interest-based activity suggestions
- Integrate campus event discovery by interest

**Trust:**
- Add video call capability for verification
- Include UQ student verification
- Show profile photo verification status
