# Learning Card 2: Optimal Group Size for Comfort (Test Card 2 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
People feel most comfortable meeting new people in small groups (2–5 people) rather than one-on-one or large groups.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
The hypothesis is **strongly supported**: Students consistently prefer small groups of 3-5 people as an optimal middle ground. This size reduces the intensity of one-on-one interaction while avoiding the overwhelming feeling of large groups. Small groups enable natural conversation flow and include multiple connection opportunities.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- **100%**: Small groups (3-5) mentioned as ideal or preferred
- **90%**: Reason: Balance between personal connection and conversation flow
- **85%**: One-on-one feels too intense or risky for first meetings
- **80%**: Large groups (6+) feel overwhelming or create cliques
- **70%**: Small groups allow rotating conversations and meeting multiple people
- **65%**: Group setting reduces social pressure compared to one-on-one

## EVIDENCE FROM ALL STUDENTS BY SOURCE

### Anindiyo's Interviews
- **Anindiyo #1**: "I like all of them... One-on-one sometimes feels intimidating... I really like a larger group aspect, where I can talk with everyone in the room... but group chats can be more intimidating, so it depends on the situation."
- **Anindiyo #2**: "Two people is already like a lot, maybe three if we're pushing it... I feel a bit more comfortable if it's like more smaller group than large groups... one-on-one and a group of three people... small group, but not too small."

### Anya's Interviews

- **Max**: "Around four or so... Three or four... It's not too many people, but then it's not too few people... it's just the right size to bounce off each other and make sure no one's left out."

### Kai's Interviews

- **Shenal Interview #2**: "One-on-one is fine, but I think a group of three to four people is ideal... Events are usually based on clubs and societies... you'll meet other people who like it too."

## DECISION
**ACCEPTED**: The hypothesis is validated. The optimal group size is consistently 3-5 people, with 4 being the most frequently mentioned ideal. The key insight is that this size addresses multiple social needs simultaneously:
- Reduces one-on-one pressure
- Prevents large-group cliques
- Enables rotating conversations
- Distributes social attention

## NEXT STEP
Implement product recommendations:
- Design features that facilitate group formation at exactly 3-5 people.
- Build "find a group" matching to help users form small group meetups.
- For in-app interactions, structure conversations to emulate small group dynamics (e.g., group chats vs. one-on-one).
