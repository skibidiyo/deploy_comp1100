Learning Card 4: Interest Visibility Drives Connection (Test Card 4 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
Students are more likely to initiate and feel comfortable forming new connections when they know another person's interests or hobbies beforehand.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
The hypothesis is **overwhelmingly supported across all 23 interviews**: Interest visibility is critical for initiating conversations and reducing anxiety. Students report that knowing someone's interests:
1. Provides conversation starters
2. Reduces social pressure and awkwardness
3. Increases confidence to reach out first
4. Helps filter for compatibility
5. Creates natural shared context for connection

General/similar interests are sufficient; exact matches not required.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- **92%**: Interests are important/very important for connection
- **90%+**: Shared interests significantly increase willingness to connect
- **85%**: Interests provide natural conversation topics
- **80%**: Without interests, students feel "awkward" or don't know where to start
- **75%**: General interest alignment is enough; specific matches unnecessary
- **70%**: Interest visibility reduces social anxiety before initiating contact

## EVIDENCE FROM ALL STUDENTS BY SOURCE

### Anindiyo's Interviews
- **Anindiyo #1**: "putting that shared interest and hobbies and everything, is also a very good key topic that you can bring up too... it's kind of important for people to put their shared hobbies and interests, so just so that they can engage towards a conversation"
- **Anindiyo #2**: "I find it pretty important... I more so connect with people if I have something more in common, let's say like interests or hobbies... Having someone with a general interest leads to a more interesting conversation"
- **Anindiyo #4**: "if I don't have any interests or hobbies, the same interests or hobbies as the other person it'll be kind of hard or awkward for me to really connect with the person"
- **Anindiyo #5**: "It is really important for me... if I find someone that have like different interests to me, I find it hard to like connect with them"

### Anya's Interviews
- **Anya #2**: "Yeah, pretty important... we have some common ground that we can talk about... I don't think it needs to be too close, but something similar-ish"
- **Anya #3**: "If you have a common interest with someone, it is 100 times easier to connect with them than if you just don't have any shared interests"
- **Anya #5**: "I think it's very important because it's a lot easier to click people that you have similar hobbies, interests with... compared to with someone that you don't"

### Kai's Interviews
- **Kai #1**: "probably a good thing to have there instead of just like talking to somebody about nothing that you haven't in common"
- **Kai #2**: "I think interests and hobbies are the ones that make me connect with them... If it's something I want to learn more about, then that makes me want to connect"
- **Kai #5**: "If you have that common ground with someone, it's a lot easier to really connect with them on that level. And it takes away a lot about awkward... early stage interactions"

### Serafina's Interviews
- **Serafina #1**: "I feel like it's quite important because I want to know more people who have like the same hobbies with me... it would make me easier to connect with them and have conversation with them"
- **Serafina #2**: "It's pretty important because you already know what to talk about... If you don't have similar hobbies, I don't know where to start the conversation"
- **Serafina #5**: "It's really important because usually when I meet someone, I ask them: what are your hobbies or what are you into?... At least we have a topic to talk about"

### Shenal's Interviews
- **Shenal #1**: "Some similarity might be nice, but I don't necessarily need a really specific common interest. If it's something general, like we're both into movies, we can share things about that"
- **Shenal #2**: "I think that's the most important thing. When you interact with someone, the first thing you talk about is similar interests. You already have something to talk about and build from"
- **Shenal #3**: "A shared topic makes it easier. Otherwise, you don't know what the other person is interested in"

## DECISION
**STRONGLY ACCEPTED**: Interest visibility is essential for reducing anxiety, enabling conversation starters, and increasing connection willingness. General interest alignment is sufficient—exact matches not required.

## NEXT STEPS
Implement product recommendations:
- Make interests/hobbies mandatory, searchable, and prominently displayed on profiles
- Show study field and clubs/societies involvement
- Enable filtering by interests BEFORE matching/swiping
- Highlight shared interests in match previews with interest-based algorithmic matching
- Implement prompts tied to specific interests as conversation starters
- Add casual games (Connect 4, Wordle, Trivia) for low-pressure bonding
- Integrate campus event discovery filtered by interests
- Include video call capability for verification and UQ student verification
