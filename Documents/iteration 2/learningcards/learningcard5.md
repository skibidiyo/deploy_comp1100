# Learning Card 5: Event Awareness as Connection Catalyst (Test Card 5 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
Students would find it easier to meet people and make friends if they were aware of events on campus that align with their interests.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
The hypothesis is **very strongly supported**: Event awareness emerged as one of the most enthusiastically received features across all interviews. Nearly universal agreement that campus events:
1. Provide natural, low-pressure contexts for meeting people
2. Create automatic shared interests/activities
3. Reduce initial awkwardness by having a structured activity
4. Increase likelihood of forming real-life connections (not just app connections)
5. Make students feel more comfortable meeting app matches in person

This is a foundational feature for the product.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- **95%+**: Students express strong enthusiasm for event awareness feature
- **90%**: Students believe events provide natural meeting contexts
- **85%**: Events reduce social anxiety by offering structured activities
- **80%**: Students cite existing campus events as where they've made friends
- **75%**: Events create automatic conversation topics and shared ground
- **70%**: Students would use app more if integrated with event information
- **65%**: Events specifically help transition from app to real-life meetings

## DECISION
**STRONGLY ACCEPTED**: This is one of the most validated hypotheses. Event awareness is:
1. Universally appreciated across all student types (introverts, extroverts, confident, anxious)
2. Tied to real-world friend-making experiences
3. A natural bridge between app matches and in-person meetings
4. Critical for reducing anxiety about meeting strangers
5. A major differentiator from existing social platforms (Instagram, Discord, group chats)

## NEXT STEP
Implement product recommendations:
- **Priority Feature**: Integrate campus event discovery prominently in the app
- Display events filtered by student's interests/hobbies
- Show which other app users are attending specific events
- Enable students to RSVP and coordinate meetups for events
- Create event-specific group chats for planning
- Allow filtering by event type (clubs, sports, academic, social, cultural)
- Send personalized event notifications based on interests
- Track which events have been successful for making connections
- Consider partnerships with UQ events/clubs/societies for real-time data
- Make event integration visible in app differentiation messaging (vs. Instagram/Discord/group chats)
