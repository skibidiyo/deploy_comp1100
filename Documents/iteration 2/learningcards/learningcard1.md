# Learning Card 1: Swipe-Based Friend Matching (Test Card 1 hypothesis)

## ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS
University students would be comfortable using a swipe-based mechanic — similar to Tinder — to discover and match with potential friends on campus.

## WHAT WE LEARNED FROM ALL INTERVIEWS
Students are open to swiping to find friends, but only when profiles show interests and info — not just photos. Photo-only swiping is rejected as judging by looks. Students clearly separate a friend-finding app from a dating app.

## THEMATIC EVIDENCE
- ~70%: Open to the swipe mechanic for friend-finding
- ~85%: Reject photo-only swiping
- ~90%: Accept swiping when profiles include interests and hobbies
- ~80%: Value mutual matching as a pressure reducer
- ~70%: Say UQ email verification is important for trust

## EVIDENCE FROM ALL STUDENTS BY SOURCE

### Anindiyo's Interviews
- **Anindiyo #1**: "You're basically selecting who you're being friends with, and you're in control." (positive)
- **Anindiyo #2**: "I think it could work… making friends and dating have some similarities." (open)

### Anya's Interviews
- **Rory**: "If I saw something I was also interested in, that could encourage me to swipe on them." (conditional)
- **Max**: "Quick and easy way to find commonalities and friends… knowing they are verified is important." (positive)

### Kai's Interviews
- **Kai #1**: "It sort of takes off the edge of the initial social interaction." (positive)
- **Kai #2**: "Interests and hobbies are the ones that make me connect with them." (interests-focused)

### Serafina's Interviews
- **Serafina #1**: "It's actually very cool… I know people have preferences." (positive)
- **Serafina #2**: "Judging people too fast from photos… [with interests added:] I think it's good." (hesitant → positive)

### Shenal's Interviews
- **Shenal #1**: "Probably not [for me]… but in an online scenario, common interests would help." (personal pass, sees value)
- **Shenal #2**: "Bumble has a BFF mode… something like that is very important for new students." (positive)

## DECISION
**CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED**: The swipe mechanic works, but only with interest-rich profiles, UQ email verification, and mutual matching. Photo-only swiping is not acceptable. App must be clearly positioned as friend-finding, not dating.

## NEXT STEP
- Show interests, hobbies, and study field prominently on swipe cards — not just photos.
- Require UQ email verification for all users.
- Implement mutual matching (both must swipe right before connecting).
- Clearly distinguish from dating apps in UI and onboarding.
