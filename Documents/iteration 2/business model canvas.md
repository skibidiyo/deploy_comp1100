## Customer Segments

General
- University students (all year levels, with priority on first-year and new students)

Specifically
- Introverted or socially anxious students (high priority)
- New/international students feeling disconnected from campus
- Students struggling with conversation initiation
- Students wanting small-group friendships (3-5 people)
- Students seeking interest-based connections (not dating)
- Students looking to attend campus events with like-minded people
- University clubs/societies (secondary - for event discovery and community integration)

## Value propositions
- **Interest-based matching with verified profiles** - Not just photos; show hobbies, interests, and study field to reduce awkwardness and increase matching confidence
- **Small-group friend-finding (3-5 people optimal)** - Reduces one-on-one pressure while avoiding large-group cliques
- **Campus event integration** - Discover events aligned with interests and meet app matches in natural, low-pressure settings
- **Conversation scaffolding** - Prompts, shared interests, and games (Wordle, Connect 4) reduce social anxiety and help start conversations
- **Safety & verification** - UQ email verification, photo verification, and video calls build trust before in-person meetings
- **Gamification for bonding** - Low-pressure games and iMessage-style activities help users connect before meeting
- **Friend-focused, not dating** - Positioned specifically as friend-finding, clearly distinct from dating apps
- **It's better because** we address the specific social anxiety and friction of meeting new people on campus through interest visibility, small-group dynamics, and campus context—unlike Instagram, Discord, or dating apps

## Channels
- Mobile app (primary - for swiping, gaming, event discovery, notifications)
- Website (secondary - account setup, browser access)
- Campus partnerships (O-week, clubs/societies, orientation)
- Student communications and campus events
- Word-of-mouth from early user adoption

## Customer relationship
- **Profile creation & discovery**: Interest-based swiping with prominent interests, hobbies, study field, and club involvement
- **Gamification**: Daily streaks, casual games (Wordle, Connect 4, Trivia), and low-pressure bonding activities
- **Interest scaffolding**: Prompts tied to shared interests, conversation starters ("I also love [shared interest]"), and shared topic suggestions
- **Event integration**: Campus event discovery filtered by interests, RSVP tracking, and meetup coordination
- **Small-group facilitation**: Matching that encourages 3-5 person meetups, group chat features, and group activity suggestions
- **Trust building**: Video call capability, photo verification, and UQ student verification before in-person meetings
- **Personalized engagement**: Interest-based recommendations, event notifications, and activity suggestions keep users active

## Revenue streams
- **Primary: Free-to-use model** (Core matching, messaging, games, and event discovery free - users show strong resistance to premium features like Tinder)
- University partnerships for prominent placement at O-week, orientation, and campus events
- Partnerships with University Clubs and Societies for event data integration and co-marketing
- Non-intrusive monetization (future, if needed): optional cosmetic features or premium profile boosts—not gatekeeping core features
