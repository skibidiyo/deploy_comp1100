**Interview Questions**
“What kind of group size feels most comfortable to you when meeting new people?”

**HYPOTHESIS**
People feel most comfortable meeting new people in small groups (2–5 people) rather than one-on-one or large groups.

**TEST**
Ask the participant: “What kind of group size feels most comfortable to you when meeting new people?”
Probe for context and reasoning: “Why do you prefer that size?”
Note any conditions (e.g., environment, familiarity, type of event).

**METRIC**
Stated preferred group size (e.g., 1, 2–5, 6–10, 10+ people)
Confidence in their preference (High / Medium / Low)
Key reasons for preference
Any specific conditions or preferences

**CRITERIA**
We are right if the majority of participants indicate that small groups (2–5 people) feel most comfortable when meeting new people, and can articulate why.

**WE ARE WRONG IF**
A significant number of participants (e.g., >50%) indicate that they feel most comfortable in one-on-one settings or large groups (6+ people) when meeting new people, or if their reasons suggest discomfort with small groups.
