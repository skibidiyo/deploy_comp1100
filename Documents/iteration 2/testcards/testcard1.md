## Hypothesis 6: Swipe-Based Friend Matching

**HYPOTHESIS**
University students would be comfortable using a swipe-based mechanic — similar to Tinder — to discover and match with potential friends on campus.

**TEST**
To verify this, we will interview university students about their familiarity with swipe-based apps and their openness to applying that mechanic to friendship-finding.

**METRIC**
We are right if:
- The majority of students are open to swiping to find friends
- Most feel that mutual matching reduces pressure

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:
- At least 60% of students are open to the swipe mechanic for making friends
- The majority feel mutual matching (both sides must say yes) reduces pressure compared to cold approaches
- Students distinguish clearly between a friend-finding swipe app and a dating app

We are wrong if:
- Most students associate swiping exclusively with dating and feel uncomfortable applying it to friendship
- Students prefer another discovery method (e.g., browsing, search, or group placement)

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. Have you heard of or used apps like Tinder or Bumble? How do you feel about the idea of swiping on people to make friends?
