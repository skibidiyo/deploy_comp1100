## Hypothesis: Event awareness facilitates real-life connections

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students would find it easier to meet people and make friends if they were aware of events on campus that align with their interests.

**TEST**
To verify this, we will ask students if knowing about nearby or upcoming events related to their interests would help them meet people in real life, and how.

**METRIC**
We are right if:
- A significant majority (more than 50%) of students confirm that event awareness would help them meet people.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:
- Students express that this would provide a natural context for interaction.
- Students believe it offers a common ground for starting conversations.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. Would knowing about nearby or upcoming events at campus related to your interests help you meet people in real life? How so?
