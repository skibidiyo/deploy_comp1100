Interview 2
Q: So what usually stops you from approaching or talking to new people?
A: I mean, maybe the environment. For example, like if we’re in a lecture hall, I probably wouldn’t go up to you. But in a smaller setting, like a classroom, maybe I would. There’s a higher chance of me trying to talk to you.

Q: So you prefer smaller groups?
A: Yeah.

Q: What has helped you the most in building a network of friends?
A: I think probably, at least in UQ, events. Most of the friends I meet are through UQ events like MSE or iftar. I just keep going, then I meet a bunch of familiar faces, and then eventually we become friends. So maybe meeting people through regular events is nice.

Q: Have you been going to a lot of events?
A: Yeah, and from there I also went to some other events too.

Q: What makes it hard to approach other students?
A: Sometimes if they’re already in a group, you don’t really want to approach them because you feel like they already have their own friends. If not, then maybe it’s just like a personal thing, like they don’t look friendly.

Q: What would make it easier for you to build friendships or find a community at uni?
A: You need shared interests. So going to clubs, and making the clubs easy to go to, like making them beginner-friendly.

Q: What is lacking from finding friends via clubs or university events?
A: From the events I’ve been going to, even though they exist, they’re not very structured. You kind of just attend and go through the motions. They don’t really have proper introductions or let you introduce yourself to people around you.

Q: Would you prefer connecting one-on-one or in groups, and why?
A: Maybe not one-on-one, but definitely a smaller group. If it’s a big group, it might be more intimidating. But I think for some people, a big group might feel less pressure.

Q: What kind of information would you want to know about someone before deciding to connect?
A: Maybe their hobbies or interests. Also maybe language, because if you can’t really communicate, it might be hard. So hobbies is one.

Q: Have you used any apps, group chats, or platforms to connect with people? What did you dislike about them?
A: I use Telegram and WeChat. My roommates use WeChat, my Singaporean friends use Telegram, and others use WhatsApp or Instagram. One thing that’s difficult is that not everybody uses the same messaging app.

Q: If you could design an app to help you make friends, what features would you want it to have?
A: Maybe something like Tinder, with profiles where you can just find people. I can’t really think of any new features.

Q: What would make you feel comfortable reaching out to someone new through an app?
A: Maybe if there was some way to guarantee that they would reply if you message first. I think people hesitate because they don’t know if they’ll get a response.
