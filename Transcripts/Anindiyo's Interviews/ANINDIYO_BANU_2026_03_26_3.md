Interview 3

[Recording][1]


Q: What usually stops you from approaching or talking to new people?
A: Um, honestly, it’s mostly overthinking. I started like wondering if I’ll sound awkward or if they are not interested. So I end up not approaching at all.

Q: What has helped you the most in building a network of friends so far?
A: Well, being in the same class as and doing group assignments helped a lot. So, like, it’s easier to talk when you already have something in common.

Q: What makes it hard to approach other students?
A: Sometimes people look busy or already in their own groups, so it feels like I might be interrupting or not welcome.

Q: What would make it easier for you to build friendships or find community at uni?
A: I think more casual and low pressure events will help, like small group activities or icebreaker, sessions where everyone is expected to talk.

Q: What is lacking from finding friends via clubs or university events?
A: You know, sometimes it feels a bit forced or too formal. Also, it’s hard to actually continue the connection up to the event and these actual things.

Q: Would you prefer connecting one on one or in groups? Why?
A: I prefer being one on one. Because it feels less intimidating and easier to build a connection.

Q: What kind of information would you wanna know about someone before deciding to connect?
A: Um, things like their interest, hobbies, maybe their course, or your level, just enough to, like, know if we have something in common.

Q: Have you used any apps or group chats or any platforms to connect with other people?
A: Uh, yeah, mainly group chats and sometimes apps.

Q: What did you dislike about the app?
A: I mean, you can feel a bit superficial or inactive. Sometimes people don’t reply or conversation just die quickly.

Q: If you could design an app to help you make friends, what features would you want to have and why?
A: I think I want a matching system based on interest on schedules, because small group meetups, instead of just chatting, also maybe pumps or guided conversations, so it’s not awkward to start.

Q: What would make you feel comfortable reaching out to someone new through an app?
A: Well, if I knew we had shared interests and if the app gave a reason to connect, like suggesting a topic or activity, also a friendly and low pressure environment will have a lot.
