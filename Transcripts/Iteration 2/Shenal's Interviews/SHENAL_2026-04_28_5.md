**Interviewer:** Hello, do I have your consent for this short interview? 
**Participant:** Yeah, go for it.

**Interviewer:** Alright, thanks. So basically I’m going to ask you around 10 questions about an app my team and I are developing for our group project. It’s about your peer circle and making new friends. You all good? Ready to go ahead?
**Participant:** Yeah.

**Interviewer:** Have you heard or used apps like Tinder or Bumble before?
**Participant:** Yeah.

**Interviewer:** So you’re familiar with how they work, like swiping on people you want to connect with?
**Participant:** Yeah, I’m familiar with that.

**Interviewer:** How would you feel about an app like that, but instead of dating, it’s for making friends?
**Participant:** It could be useful depending on how the app is made.

**Interviewer:** If you were to use an app like that, is it important for you to know someone’s interests or hobbies before you decide to connect with them?
**Participant:** Yeah, I’d say it’s pretty important because common interests play a huge role in building friendships. So yeah, it’s very important.

**Interviewer:** Do those interests need to be aligned with yours, or are you open to exploring new interests?
**Participant:** I’d say similar interests matter more, but that probably changes from person to person. Personally, I think similar interests matter more.

**Interviewer:** What kind of information would you like to see on someone’s profile that would make you feel more comfortable connecting with them?
**Participant:** Probably a bio stating their interests and hobbies, so I know the kind of people I want to connect with.

**Interviewer:** Would you also want to see things like age, gender, and background?
**Participant:** Yeah, yeah.

**Interviewer:** When meeting new people, what kind of group size feels most comfortable for you?
**Participant:** I think a group setting makes the most sense. Anywhere from three to five people is a good sweet spot so it’s not awkward and there’s always something to talk about.

**Interviewer:** Do you find it helpful when there’s already a shared topic to talk about instead of starting from scratch?
**Participant:** Yeah, it helps avoid awkward silences and makes connecting with people a lot easier.

**Interviewer:** How would you feel about getting reminders from the app about upcoming events at your uni related to your interests?
**Participant:** I’d say it’s pretty helpful, especially if it connects you with people who have common interests. It makes it easier to build connections and make new friends.

**Interviewer:** What kind of features would make you use the app regularly or keep coming back to it?
**Participant:** Not really, I don’t have any specific ideas about that.

**Interviewer:** Would you be open to doing a mini activity, like a game, when you first meet someone instead of just chatting?
**Participant:** Depends. If it’s after you’ve talked a bit, that’s fine. But if it’s the first thing, that might feel awkward.

**Interviewer:** Do you have any complaints or negative things about existing apps that we could improve?
**Participant:** I’d say highlight common interests when viewing someone’s profile. On apps like Instagram, you don’t really know what someone’s into until you talk to them. Highlighting shared interests would make it easier to connect.

**Interviewer:** Alright, thanks. You’ve been a lot of help. That’s all the questions I have.
**Participant:** No worries.
