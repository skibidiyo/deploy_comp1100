**Interviewer:** Hello, do I have your consent for a short interview?
**Participant:** Yeah, sure, go ahead.

**Interviewer:** Alright, thank you. So basically, I’m going to ask you around 10 questions about a group project my team and I are doing. It’s about creating a peer programme. Are you ready for this?
**Participant:** Yeah, sure.

**Interviewer:** Have you heard of or used apps like Tinder or Bumble before?
**Participant:** I’ve heard of them, but I haven’t personally used them.

**Interviewer:** Okay, but you are familiar with the idea, right? Like, you swipe on people that you like.
**Participant:** Yeah, yeah.

**Interviewer:** How would you feel about an app like that, but instead of dating, it’s for making friends?
**Participant:** It’s convenient, but I think it can be a little bit shallow for friendships. Operating it makes sense, but I’m not sure.

**Interviewer:** How important is it for you to know someone’s interests or hobbies before deciding to connect with them?
**Participant:** I feel like it’s pretty important. It makes it way easier to start a conversation. If you already have something in common, it’s less awkward.

**Interviewer:** Do their interests need to align with yours, or are you open to exploring new interests?
**Participant:** I think generally similar interests are enough to start with. For example, being into tech or gaming. But having at least one specific overlap helps keep the conversation going.

**Interviewer:** What kind of information would make you feel more comfortable connecting with someone on an app?
**Participant:** Clear interests and a short bio with some personality. Also something easy to comment on, like what they’re currently working on. It just makes starting a conversation feel more natural.

**Interviewer:** What group size feels most comfortable when meeting new people?
**Participant:** Probably a small group at first, like two to five people.

**Interviewer:** So you prefer group meetups over one-on-ones?
**Participant:** Yeah, it depends, but generally that would be fine.

**Interviewer:** Do you find it helpful when there’s already something to talk about, like a shared topic?
**Participant:** Yeah, definitely. Starting from scratch can feel awkward. Having a shared topic or prompt makes it much easier to break the ice.

**Interviewer:** How would you feel about getting notifications about upcoming or nearby events through the app?
**Participant:** I think it gives a natural way to meet people in real life instead of it being awkward. Since we’re all in college, it’s also good for networking—not just socialising. It could help with internships, research, and other professional opportunities.

**Interviewer:** Are there any features you think current apps are missing that we could add?
**Participant:** I feel like most current social apps are mainly for people you already know. It would be better if it focused more on actually meeting new people in real life, instead of just chatting online. That’s where it should stand out.

**Interviewer:** Alright, thank you so much. That’s all the questions I’ve got for you.
**Participant:** Yeah, no worries.

**Interviewer:** Thank you. Take care.
