Interview 5

[Recording][1]


Q: What usually stops you from approaching or talking to new people?
A: I guess it would probably be, like... what stops me usually? Myself, I guess, like, me being an introvert person makes it hard to approach new people. Like, I have to figure out what topics or conversation I need to come up with before approaching them. It’s usually very a template type of conversation, so... yeah.

Q: What has helped you the most in building network are friends so far?
A: I guess, like, clubs, actually. Like, I joined, uh, dance clubs here, and also, ’cause I’m a foreigner here. I joined the Indonesian community club. That’s also very helpful, you know, to build network and make more friends there. So, yeah, because, like, most of the Indonesian people here that has already been here for quite a while, way before I was here, they made a lot of foreign friends. I get to make foreign friends through them also. So that’s pretty fun.

Q: What makes it hard to approach other students?
A: It would probably, like, if I already try to approach these new people, they sometimes, you know, can tend, they tend to be guarded too, you know? So, it’s hard to pick the right ones who are, like, open to making new friends sometimes.

Q: What would make it easier for you to build friendships or find community at uni?
A: Hmm, that will make it easier. Probably, like, find some commonalities, like, same hobbies, maybe, or, like, same activities that we like to do. That’s, like, such an easy connection to make with that.

Q: You said you joined the dance club and the Indonesian Club—what is lacking from finding friends via clubs?
A: It would probably be, like, for dance clubs, like, they tend to join these clubs solely for dancing and not for networking. Which tends to be a little bit annoying sometimes, you know? That could be one of the reasons, and as for, like, the Asian clubs, they kind of they tend to already have their own circle. ’Cause, like, they came in a batch or something, so, like, you know, there’s already a circle in a circle, so it’s kind of hard to get into one of the circles when you’re new.

Q: Would you prefer connecting one-on-one or in groups?
A: I would prefer in groups, actually, ’cause, you know, everyone’s... if it’s, like, everyone is new to the community, it’s more interactive and engaging. Like there’s no, like, awkward silence or something like that, you know? I think group is more fun. I’m actually more scared if it’s groups.

Q: Why is that?
A: Really? ’Cause it’s more people. I feel like if there’s a lot, like, I’m also introverted, so, like, there’s a lot of people, they tend to talk over me, and I can, like, I use that time to, like, think of something.

Q: What kind of information would you want to know about someone before deciding to connect?
A: Really, like, what do you study? Because, like, you know, for example, you in computer... I don’t know anything about computer science, so that’s something that is interesting to know, like, differences also can be a good topic of conversation, like getting to know your background, and also, again, what they like to do as, like, a hobby.

Q: Have you used any apps, group chats, or platforms to connect with people?
A: Of course, Instagram, obviously.

Q: On Instagram?
A: Yeah. Uh... Instagram. And also, sometimes from Instagram, we tend to move to, like, LINE, or, like, Snapchat. Also, Snapchat is very big here.

Q: What did you dislike about Instagram or these platforms?
A: Probably... nothing much actually. I much prefer Instagram rather than Snapchat, ’cause in Snapchat, you can, like, instantly block them. Like, once you’re blocked, you don’t know that you’re blocked, you know what I mean? Whereas, like, on Instagram, you can see, like, if you’re being blocked or unfollowed. You can still reach out to them more, as Snapchat—you can’t, once you’re blocked or removed.

Q: If you could design an app to help you make friends, what features would you want it to have and why?
A: Um... it’s actually an app that has already existed. I forgot the name, but basically, you can just, like, create an event, like, a side quest or something, and people can just join and follow, like, you know. I’ve seen that app. Right? I forgot the name. It’s, like, M something, but, yeah, I’ve used it once with my friends, and I’ve made a ton of new friends ever since. So, yeah, that’s pretty fun.

Q: What would make you feel comfortable reaching out to someone using an app?
A: Probably, like, the app that I just said before, because we set, like, we made an activity, and when people come and join this activity, automatically, we assume that, oh, you like this type of side quest, too. So, like, we have something in common then, you know? That’s a very good, like, startup conversation.
